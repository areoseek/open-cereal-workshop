# Sounds and presets: where they may come from

Nothing from the reference product ships here. Each pack we add gets a row: name, source URL, licence, attribution text.

Accepted licences: CC0 / public domain, CC BY (with the attribution kept here and in the app's credits), and packs whose own licence allows redistribution inside open source software.
Not accepted: CC BY-NC (blocks commercial use by our users' distributors), "royalty free" packs that forbid redistribution of the raw files, anything with no stated licence.

Presets for our own instruments are written by us. Synthesized drums and wavetables are generated by code in this repository.
